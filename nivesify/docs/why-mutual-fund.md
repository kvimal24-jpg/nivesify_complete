# Why Mutual Fund

## Overview
The Why Mutual Fund page educates users about the benefits of mutual funds, including diversification, professional management, and accessibility. It provides context for new investors and links to analysis and selection tools.

## User Flow
- User visits the Why Mutual Fund page.
- Reads about mutual fund advantages and common myths.
- Can navigate to analysis, Smart Fund Finder, or Quick Picks.
